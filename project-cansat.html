cansat

A soda-can-sized satellite payload, designed, built and launched by a four-person student team for the CSDC Advanced competition.

role
Team lead
team
4 people
when
Sep 2025 – Apr 2026
budget
$500 CAD
built with
ESP32, 915 MHz LoRa radio, I2C / SPI sensors, CAD, hand soldering
result
Telemetry at 4 Hz with zero dropouts in pre-launch testing; launched in Lethbridge, AB.

what i did

  • Led the team through design, a $500 CAD budget, testing and launch, and ran task delegation, documentation and milestone tracking.
  • Planned the payload sensor suite: temperature, pressure, humidity, CO2, IMU and GPS.
  • Modelled the shell assembly in CAD.
  • Assembled all components onto the flight board.

what went wrong

The custom PCB failed on arrival. I hand-soldered half of the flight board myself so the team stayed on schedule for launch.

result

The ESP32 payload streamed telemetry over 915 MHz LoRa at 4 Hz with zero dropouts in pre-launch testing.
