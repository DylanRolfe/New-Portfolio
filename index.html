about

i'm dylan rolfe. from vancouver and london, uk, now based in waterloo, studying mechatronics engineering @ uwaterloo.

looking for summer 2027 internships. i design and build integrated hardware and software systems: a satellite payload with live lora telemetry, a 150 g combat robot, and a 3d campus map used by over 1,000 people.
