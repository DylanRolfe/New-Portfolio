habguard-lite

A design for an add-on that doses powdered activated carbon automatically when a harmful algal bloom reaches a desalination plant’s intake, replacing manual guesswork.

status
Design proposal
field
Seawater desalination pretreatment

the problem

Harmful algal blooms load seawater with sticky organic matter that fouls reverse-osmosis membranes. The write-up estimates around 150 large plants are affected each year, losing 4 to 15 days per event. Powdered activated carbon helps, but most plants that use it dose it by hand, which is error-prone and labour-intensive.

the design

  • Sensors at the intake read UV254, fluorescence, chlorophyll-a and turbidity to catch a bloom early.
  • A controller compares the readings with thresholds and switches the plant into “bloom mode” when they are crossed.
  • In bloom mode it calculates the carbon dose and raises it gradually, inside minimum, maximum and safety limits.
  • A feeder adds the dose ahead of the mixing tank, and a dashboard logs the signals, doses and savings for operators.

estimated impact

By the proposal’s own estimate, automating dosing and making carbon practical for more plants could save 77,500 to 924,000 cubic metres of water a year.
